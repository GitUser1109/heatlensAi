# Data notes

`demo_synthetic.csv` is generated by `scripts/train_model.py`. It is **not observed environmental data** and does not describe real locations in Mumbai. It exists to make the app runnable immediately and to demonstrate model training, inference, evaluation, and explainability.

When replacing it, document source URL, acquisition date, spatial resolution, units, quality filters, missing-data handling, and license/attribution. Do not mix surface temperature with air temperature as if they were the same variable.
